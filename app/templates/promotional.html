FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions before visiting IdeaHub? Find quick answers about our spaces, reservations, pricing, and what you can expect.

How can I book a space?

You can contact IdeaHub directly to ask about space availability and booking arrangements.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Cash, GCash, BPI, and BDO payments for your space and food orders.

What makes the spaces different from each other?

The Regular Lounge and Premium Lounge are suited for work or study, while the Boardroom is designed for meetings and group activities.

How long can I use a space?

You can use a space based on the length of time you need. Space charges are calculated according to how long you use the space.

Can I study or work while enjoying food and drinks?

Yes. You can work or study in the space while enjoying food and drinks from the café.

How much does it cost to use a space?

Space rates vary depending on the space you choose. Check the listed rate for the Regular Lounge, Premium Lounge, or Boardroom.
