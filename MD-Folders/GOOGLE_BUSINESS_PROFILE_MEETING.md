6. Keep or assign the business owner as **Primary owner**.
7. Invite my Google account as **Manager**, if desired.
8. Open **Edit profile → Business information → Contact**.
9. Set the website to `https://idea-flows.online/`.
10. Correct the address, phone number, hours, and open/closed status if needed.
11. Save the changes and check whether Google marks any edits as pending.

- A newly added owner must wait seven days before receiving some ownership-management capabilities.
- Connecting the profile improves Google’s understanding, but it does not guarantee an immediate top ranking.
