# Daily Balance: readable reconciliation, reports, and soft balances

**Status: implemented locally on October 25, 2026.** Production deployment is pending a separate user request.

## Goal

Make Daily Balance readable without horizontal scrolling on phones or desktop. Keep every financial amount available for review while showing only the most useful figures first. Apply this to payment method reconciliation, Daily Reports, AM/PM soft balancing history, and Cash boxes by Manila day. Simplify PDF, Excel, and CSV exports too.

## Agreed design choices

- Show a short summary for each payment method; tap or click for the full breakdown.
- Hide payment methods whose amounts are all zero by default. Provide **Show all methods**. If all are zero, show a clear empty state.
- Show Daily Reports and AM/PM soft balances as expandable cards: key totals on the closed card; all existing details, notes, and recorder information inside.
- Include Cash boxes by Manila day in the no-side-scroll redesign.
- Simplify on-page views and all three exports. Change CSV to a narrow row-based format, even though this changes its previous column schema.

## Current layout and data flow

- `app/templates/admin/daily_balance.html` renders a 15-column payment method table, a 24-column Daily Reports table, a 15-column soft balance table, and a nine-column cash box table. `white-space: nowrap` and Bootstrap `.table-responsive` force sideways scrolling.
- `renderFilteredReports()` accumulates totals from `reportsCache` for the selected Manila date range and fills the payment, report, and cash-box tables. `renderFilteredSoftBalances()` fills the AM/PM table. The date filter and API errors already have separate handling.
- `app/services/sales_service.py` and `app/repositories/sales_repository.py` supply the live ledger values. This redesign should consume those same API fields and not change ledger formulas, business dates, snapshots, or payment history.
- `app/services/daily_balance_export_service.py` builds CSV, PDF context, and Excel. The PDF template is `app/templates/admin/daily_balance_report_pdf.html`. Current tests assert some wide CSV headers and fixed Excel column positions, so export tests must be updated deliberately.

## Proposed on-page layout

### 1. Payment method reconciliation

Use one compact card per nonzero method for the selected date range. Show the method, **Net movement**, **Checkout sales**, **Money received**, and **Money paid out**. For this summary, money received is checkout sales **less customer credit applied**, plus credit received, customer debt collected, other income, and owner cash added. Money paid out is cash taken out, business expenses, business debt paid, budget spending, and refunds. The card's native expandable section shows every existing flow with plain labels:

- Sales/credit: checkout sales, credit applied, and credit received, kept as separate labelled figures.
- Other money received: customer debt collected, other income, and owner cash added.
- Money paid out: cash taken out, business expenses, business debt paid, budget spending, and refunds.
- Separate context: external-funded expenses and debt payments. These appear in the detail but are not subtracted a second time from business net movement. Owner reimbursement is a subset of debt paid, not another deduction.

Keep the current per-method net formula exactly as calculated by the ledger. Use the same source totals for the grouped figures so **Money received − Money paid out = Net movement**. Give positive/negative values clear text labels as well as color. Display the older-method-unknown card only when it has nonzero values. The **Show all methods** control reveals zero cards without changing totals.

### 2. Daily Reports

Replace each wide row with a card headed by its Manila date and live/saved report status. The collapsed view shows **Checkout sales**, **Debt collected**, **Business expenses**, and **Net movement**. Expand it to see the full existing set grouped as Sales (Cowork, Cafe, Discounts, payment methods, refunds), Other inflows, Outflows, Cash boxes, Activity counts, and Audit (generated by/at, notes, snapshot differences). Preserve the date-range filter and current descending date order. A date with live activity still appears even when no report snapshot was generated.

### 3. Soft Balancing History (AM/PM)

Use one card per date and period. Show revenue, expenses, net, and any live-versus-snapshot cash difference on the closed card. Expand for debt collection, other income, debt paid, budget spend, petty/customer snapshots, generated by/at, and notes. Keep AM and PM separate; never merge or recalculate saved snapshots. Highlight a nonzero difference as a review item, without changing the saved value.

### 4. Cash boxes by Manila day

Show separate Petty cash and Customer cash cards for each date. The collapsed card shows opening and closing held cash. Expand for cash received, owner top-ups, spending/refunds, cash taken out, and original snapshot difference. Retain the warning for older unassigned cash entries and the link to Cash Boxes history.

### Responsive and accessibility rules

- Use one card per row on narrow screens and a small grid on wide screens. No section above should use a wide table, fixed minimum width, clipped amount, or horizontal scrollbar.
- Reuse semantic headings and native `<details>/<summary>` for keyboard and touch access. Use labelled amount/value pairs and visible focus states. Keep amounts and labels readable at 320 px, 375 px, tablet width, and desktop width.
- Make long notes wrap. Put loading, empty, and failed-request messages in the affected section; do not turn API errors into zero amounts.

## Export layout

### PDF

Replace the broad reconciliation and report tables with short, vertically grouped sections. Start with period totals, then each active payment method, then each daily report and existing AM/PM snapshot. Put the summary first and the audit detail directly below its date/method. Keep all amounts, notes, recorder, and snapshot differences available; use page breaks to avoid splitting a small section awkwardly. Verify text and amounts are not clipped in the generated PDF.

### Excel

Keep a readable Summary sheet. Replace the extremely wide Daily Reports sheet with a compact date summary and a narrow **Report Details** sheet of labelled rows. Make Payment Methods a narrow `Date | Method | Flow | Amount` sheet, retaining all flow values and method attribution. Keep comparison and trend content if still useful, but no primary accounting sheet should require horizontal scrolling to read its figures. Preserve number formats, dates, and notes.

### CSV

Switch to a narrow schema such as `Date, Section, Item, Method, Amount, Notes` with one metric per row. Mark summary rows and detail rows explicitly so importing or summing all rows does not double-count. Include method flows, cash-box movements, audit notes, and totals already present in the old CSV. Give the download a new filename that signals the format change; document the new columns. Never drop an amount merely because it is hidden in the on-page summary.

## Accounting rules to preserve

- Use the same Asia/Manila date filter and existing API/ledger records for the page and exports. Layout changes must not change checkout revenue, debt collection, owner top-ups, outflows, refunds, or net movement.
- Keep external-funded expense/debt amounts visible but outside business cash outflow. Keep credit applied separate from new cash received. Do not count owner reimbursement twice.
- Keep live values distinct from generated report and soft-balance snapshots. Preserve generated-by, generated-at, notes, and cash snapshot differences.
- Existing nonzero method totals must reconcile to the same overall Daily Balance totals before and after the redesign. Zero-method hiding is presentation only.

## Implementation sequence

1. Add small, reusable display helpers in `daily_balance.html` for labelled amounts and safe text. Reuse `reportsCache`, `softBalancesCache`, date filters, and existing API responses. Replace the four tables one section at a time; remove table-only CSS and swipe instructions when no longer needed.
2. Restructure the PDF template and export-service Excel/CSV presentation using the same report dictionaries and `_method_rows()` calculation. Do not change repository or money-writing code unless an existing field is demonstrably missing.
3. Update export tests that depend on the old wide CSV and Excel positions. Add focused checks that a representative ledger date produces the same sales, collections, business outflows, cash-box closing balances, and net in the API, cards, PDF context, Excel, and narrow CSV. Check that retries or old snapshots are not reinterpreted.
4. Inspect the rendered page at phone, tablet, and desktop widths; confirm there is no horizontal page/section scrollbar, expansion is keyboard usable, long notes wrap, and positive/negative amounts remain readable. Render a multi-day PDF and inspect pages for clipped text. Run the relevant finance/export tests and the full suite because exports cross several money sources.

## Acceptance checks

- A user can read the selected period's nonzero payment methods, each daily report, each AM/PM soft balance, and each dated cash box without scrolling sideways.
- Tapping a card reveals every currently available amount and its label; zero methods can be shown on demand.
- The same date range produces unchanged accounting totals across the page, API, and exports. Cash boxes carry forward and snapshot differences remain visible.
- PDF fits its pages; Excel's main sheets are narrow; CSV is narrow and clearly distinguishes summary from detail rows.
- Staff and admin see the same permitted figures; errors remain visible instead of appearing as empty or zero data.

## Deployment boundary

This is a presentation and export-format change. Prepare and test it locally first. The live VPS must not be changed until the user separately requests deployment and a fresh verified production backup is available. Tell anyone importing the old CSV that its columns will change.

## Implementation notes

- All four wide on-page tables now use expandable cards. Expanded cards remain open across polling refreshes. Nonzero external-funded activity remains visible even when a method's business net movement is zero. Empty unknown methods stay hidden.
- CSV downloads as `daily_balance_details_v2.csv` with `Date, Section, Item, Method, Amount, Notes`. Amounts are numeric PHP values; count rows are labeled in Notes. Section names distinguish daily summaries, payment detail, and saved AM/PM snapshots, which overlap and must not be summed together.
- Excel has a five-column Daily Reports summary and four-column Report Details, Payment Methods, and Soft Balances sheets. Dates are typed dates, amounts are numeric, and long audit text wraps into readable continuation rows. Notes beginning with spreadsheet formula characters remain literal text.
- PDF uses portrait pages and short label/value sections, including active method detail and saved snapshots. Long words and notes wrap in both the normal template and the existing fallback renderer.
- All export formats now validate and apply the same requested Manila date range, including soft snapshots. No schema or ledger formula changed.
- Visual checks covered admin and staff, 320/375/768/1280 px widths, keyboard expansion, empty dates, zero-method toggling, polling state, and long notes. Rendered the PDF and every Excel sheet; the sample PDF had no horizontal text overflow.
- Local PDF raster checks used the existing `xhtml2pdf` fallback. The primary WeasyPrint renderer could not run on this Windows host because its native libraries are unavailable.
- Verification: 55 focused finance/export checks passed. The full suite executed 303 tests: 296 passed and seven assertions still expected the old export format. Those assertions were updated and all seven passed within a final 17-test export/ledger rerun. No unresolved failure remains from that run.
- The separate Cash Boxes modal simplification plan remains pending.
